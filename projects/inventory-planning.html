The reporting problem

Replenishment decisions need more than current stock. Allocations, inbound quantities, demand and lead time all affect whether an item should be reviewed for purchase.

Approach

  1. Used exact-match VLOOKUPs to bring item attributes into the analysis and SUMIFS/COUNTIFS to aggregate category spend and reorder flags.
  2. Calculated stock position as on hand minus allocated plus on order. Daily demand is trailing 90-day demand divided by 90.
  3. Set the reorder point from lead time plus safety days and the target from those inputs plus the review cycle. Tables, validation, conditional formatting and a native chart make the model inspectable.

FINDING → NEXT ACTION

27 items meet the reorder rule, with £766,792 of proposed purchase cost. Sinks account for the largest category proposal at about £290,239. These are scenario estimates to review, not approved purchases or cost savings.
