The reporting problem

Looking only at received orders hides overdue orders that have not arrived. A supplier report needs a consistent due-date population and measures that do not reward incomplete records.

Approach

  1. Built a four-table model with three one-to-many relationships and eight DAX measures across an overview and supplier-detail page.
  2. Calculated OTIF across all 1,488 lines due by the snapshot, including overdue unreceived lines. A successful line must arrive by its due date and meet the ordered quantity.
  3. Added supplier, item-category and due-month filters to support follow-up on delivery exceptions and lead times.

FINDING → NEXT ACTION

575 of 1,488 due order lines arrived on time and in full, giving 38.6% OTIF. Eighteen open lines are overdue. These exceptions provide a starting point for a supplier review rather than evidence of the reasons for delay.

Use the supplier-detail view to isolate overdue and short-received lines, then confirm causes and recovery dates with the supplier before recommending changes.

Scope and limitations

Snapshot: 21 September 2026. Results are line-weighted, not spend-weighted. The fixture assumes one receipt record per line. No live SAP or Power BI Service connection, scheduled cloud refresh or measured business savings are claimed.
